## Style Prompt
Dark editorial gastronomy. Deep near-black backgrounds (#0D0B09) with a Galician-forest-green accent (#4A8A52). Serif display type (Fraunces, light weight) contrasted against tight-tracking sans body. Motion is slow, weighted, cinematic — like a high-end food magazine coming to life. Images are the hero: full-bleed, warm, tactile.

## Colors
- `#0D0B09` — primary background (near-black, warm)
- `#4A8A52` — brand green (Galician oak / moss)
- `#F7F3EC` — off-white (warm paper)
- `rgba(255,255,255,0.55)` — secondary text
- `rgba(0,0,0,0.88)` — deep overlay for readability

## Typography
- **Display:** Fraunces (serif, light/300 weight) — names, headlines
- **Body/UI:** Inter (sans-serif, 400/600) — labels, categories, prices

## What NOT to Do
- No gradients except for photo overlays (bottom vignette only)
- No colorful UI chrome — let food photography carry the color
- No bold/heavy Fraunces — light weight only for elegance
- No white backgrounds — always dark
- No drop-shadows on text — use overlay gradients instead
